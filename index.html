“To us as a university community a son has been given, and the government shall be upon his shoulder. His name is TUNDE OYE MODICIOUS — The Son of the Sun.” — Biblical Allusion (Isaiah 9:6) · As declared at the Campaign Launch

Tunde Modicious Oye is more than a name on a ballot — he is a movement, a mission, and a mandate. Currently serving as the Africa Hall JCRC Vice President at KNUST, Tunde has already proven that he leads with purpose, passion, and the people at heart.

A Political Science student by training and a student advocate by calling, Tunde carries the conviction that KNUST’s SRC must evolve — from a body that merely reacts to one that proactively champions the interests of over 80,000 students.

He calls himself the “Son of the Sun” because, by grace and GODFIDENCE — a coinage of God + Confidence — he is hardly unlucky. His name, TUNDE, means Return / Rebirth in Yoruba. And that is precisely what his presidency promises.

REBIRTH
TUNDE · “One Who Returns” · A New Beginning For KNUST SRC

The word TUNDE literally means Rebirth. This campaign is not just a bid for an office — it is a declaration that the SRC’s obsolete era is over. The TUNDELISED SRC will be proactive, inclusive, and driven by the collective will of 80,000 KNUST students.

01
🏛️
Accountable Governance

Transparent SRC operations, open books, and regular town halls. A leadership that owes its existence to the students and answers to them — always.

02
⚡
Proactive Representation

Anticipating student needs before they become crises. The TUNDELISED SRC acts — it does not merely react. Every student issue is a presidential priority.

03
🤝
Inclusive Platforms

Every faculty, every hall, every student voice deserves a seat at the SRC table. Tunde’s presidency will be a presidency of ALL — not a few.

04
🌎
Student Welfare First

From mental health to academic support, from hostel conditions to campus safety — your wellbeing is the heartbeat of every policy Tunde will champion.

05

Leveraging technology to bring SRC services, updates, and engagement closer to every student — wherever they are on campus.

06
