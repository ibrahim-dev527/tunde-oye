The Meaning

WHAT IS
REBIRTH?

REBIRTH is Tunde's direct response to years of student frustration with an SRC that felt disconnected, inactive, and unresponsive to the real lives of KNUST students.

It is not just a campaign theme — it is a complete governance transformation blueprint. REBIRTH defines exactly what will change, how it will change, and when every KNUST student will feel the difference.

Under REBIRTH, the SRC transitions from a passive body that reacts — to an active, proactive force that anticipates student needs and delivers results.

"REBIRTH is the moment KNUST's student governance steps out of the past and into a future that every student deserves."

Why REBIRTH?

THE PROBLEMS
WE MUST FIX

📵
Current Problem
No Digital Presence
The KNUST SRC has no functional online portal, no digital complaint system, and no way for students to engage remotely with governance.
REBIRTH launches a full SRC Student Portal within the first 30 days.
🔒
Current Problem
Closed-Door Governance
SRC meetings, budgets, and decisions happen behind closed doors. Students are left guessing what their representatives are actually doing.
REBIRTH introduces open meetings, live-streams, and monthly published reports.
😔
Current Problem
Neglected Welfare
Student accommodation, mental health support, and campus healthcare are persistently inadequate — with no strong SRC advocacy to address them.
REBIRTH establishes a dedicated Student Welfare Desk and Emergency Fund.
🎓
Current Problem
Missed Opportunities
KNUST graduates some of Africa's best minds, yet most students leave without ever connecting to the career and business opportunities they deserve.
REBIRTH creates internship pipelines, industry expos, and an innovation fund.
💸
Current Problem
Financial Opacity
Student dues and SRC funds are managed without public transparency. Students have no way to know how their money is being used.
REBIRTH publishes all financial reports monthly on the public SRC portal.
😤
Current Problem
Broken Promises
Election promises are made and promptly forgotten. There is no mechanism to hold SRC leadership accountable to their commitments.
REBIRTH launches a live Manifesto Tracker Dashboard — every promise, publicly tracked.
