Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY
ASKED

How do I vote for Tunde?+

Voting takes place during the official KNUST SRC elections. All registered students are eligible. Watch Tunde's social media and our website for the election date announcement from the Electoral Commissioner.

How can I volunteer for the campaign?+

Visit our Join page and fill in the form — you'll be connected to the volunteer team instantly via WhatsApp. Or message us directly at 0530895680. All halls and departments welcome.

What does WONIM TUNDE mean?+

"WONIM TUNDE?" is Twi for "Do you know Tunde?" — a powerful rhetorical question challenging every KNUST student to discover who Tunde Oye Modicious is and what he stands for. It emphasizes his significance, influence, and readiness to lead.

What is the REBIRTH agenda?+

REBIRTH is Tunde's complete transformation blueprint for the KNUST SRC covering 5 pillars: Academic Excellence, Student Welfare, Digital Transformation, Transparency & Accountability, and Opportunities & Innovation. Visit the REBIRTH page for the full breakdown.

When is the SRC election date?+

The official KNUST SRC election date will be announced by the Electoral Commissioner on campus. Follow Tunde's social media channels and this website for the latest confirmed date. You can also WhatsApp us at 0530895680 for updates.

Can I request a media interview?+

Yes! For all media inquiries, press interviews, podcast appearances, or coverage requests, contact the campaign directly via WhatsApp at 0530895680 or via the Quick Message form above. We respond to all media requests promptly.
